PyFy Telemetry F1 captures F1 sim racing telemetry and turns it into corner-by-corner coaching you can act on right away: find the top 3 turns where you lose time against a reference lap, and how to fix your consistency, technique, racing lines, and ERS management to find lap time. Supports both the 2026 and 2025 regulations and telemetry formats, including Madrid. This guide covers installation, capturing telemetry, generating the analysis charts and preparing an external AI coaching request.
