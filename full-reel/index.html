Single Race vs Reference

Compare your race vs. the built-in reference. Start the walkthrough.

  1. FIND MORE PACE / TURN ANALYSIS

    Time Gained / Lost per Turn vs Reference

    Find more pace: turn by turn against the track’s built-in reference. Red is where you’re slower, green is where you’re faster. The footnote names the corners worth the most.

  2. FIND LAP TIME / TURN ANALYSIS

    Time Left on Table

    Get time back right now: the gap between your median and your best through each corner is pace you already have. Execute closer to your best, consistently, in the three turns/corners named here.

  3. TECHNIQUE / CONSISTENCY

    Braking Point Consistency

    Next, fix your technique, starting with braking points at every priority corner, lap by lap. This chart compares your median (in orange) to the reference (dashed purple). Wide spreads away from the reference mean you’re leaving lap time on the table.

  4. TECHNIQUE / CONSISTENCY

    Throttle Committed Consistency

    Where you commit to throttle on exit, lap by lap. The purple dash is the reference. Late or scattered commitment is exit speed left on the table.

  5. TECHNIQUE / RACE START

    Standing Start Analysis

    Reaction time, traction, throttle application and wheel spin off the line, plus places gained or lost into Turn 1.

  6. TECHNIQUE / RACING LINE

    Racing Line - T6

    Improve racing lines where needed. One line per lap for each priority corner, vs the purple dashed reference. The footnote names divergences worth correcting.

  7. TECHNIQUE / TRAIL BRAKING

    Brake Traces & Gear Overlay - T6

    Brake pressure, pedal release, and gear downshifts through a specific turn/corner. Priority turns are detected automatically. The fastest lap is highlighted vs the dashed purple reference. See whether you're correctly trail braking to the apex.

  8. TECHNIQUE / THROTTLE APPLICATION

    Throttle Traces - T6

    Throttle application and modulation past the apex of a specific turn/corner. Priority turns are detected automatically. The fastest lap is highlighted vs the dashed purple reference. See whether you're getting back on throttle early enough.

  9. TECHNIQUE / SPEED TRACES

    Speed Traces - T6

    Entry, minimum and exit speed through T6, lap by lap, vs the dashed purple reference.

  10. TECHNIQUE / RACING LINE

    Racing Line - T12+T13+T14

    Improve racing lines where needed. One line per lap for each priority corner, vs the purple dashed reference. The footnote names divergences worth correcting.

  11. TECHNIQUE / TRAIL BRAKING

    Brake Traces & Gear Overlay - T12+T13+T14

    Brake pressure, pedal release, and gear downshifts through the T12-T13-T14 complex. Corner boundaries and the reference apex are marked. The fastest lap is highlighted vs the dashed purple reference. See whether you're correctly trail braking to each apex.

  12. TECHNIQUE / SPEED TRACES

    Speed Traces - T12+T13+T14

    Speed through the T12-T13-T14 complex, lap by lap, vs the dashed purple reference. See where your minimum speed sits, and how much speed you carry out of each corner.

  13. ENERGY / ERS MANAGEMENT

    ERS Deployment Pattern

    Finally, optimize ERS. How much battery you deploy on the run to each corner, lap by lap, against the reference pattern (dashed purple). The straights where the reference spends more are the straights where you leave time on the table.

  14. ENERGY / ERS MANAGEMENT

    ERS Harvesting Pattern

    The other half of ERS: battery recovered into each corner, lap by lap, against the reference (dashed purple). Over-harvesting where the reference does not is speed scrubbed on entry.

  15. SESSION OVERVIEW

    Session Scorecard

    The whole session on one card: context, race start, technique scores, consistency scores, plus the two biggest opportunities (identified per turn/corner) to gain lap time or find extra pace right now.

  16. PROGRESSION / RACE VS RACE

    Time Gained / Lost per Turn vs Another Selected Race

    The reference lap is the target to chase, but you can also compare two of your own races, turn by turn, and see what improved or regressed. Pick your strongest session as the baseline, or the one you want to learn from.

  17. RACE PACE

    Clean Lap Times Progression

    Every clean lap in order, with median vs the reference. Outlier laps are detected and annotated.

  18. RACE PACE

    Clean Lap Times Per Stint

    Pace within each tyre stint: fastest, slowest and the trend. See whether you’re extracting performance from each tyre compound you used.

  19. RACE PACE

    Tyre Degradation

    Added cost per lap as tyres age: fuel-normalized lap times per stint, with compound degradation in ms/lap. Steeper than expected means you're overdriving the tyre or not handling loss of grip efficiently.

  20. TYRE MANAGEMENT

    Tyre Management

    Tyre surface and carcass temperatures versus their optimal windows, lap by lap and stint by stint.

  21. ADVANCED / FULL LAP TELEMETRY

    Fastest Lap Telemetry

    Your fastest lap plotted at high resolution: brake, throttle, steering, lateral G, speed and gear with the turn zones marked.

  22. ADVANCED / FULL LAP TELEMETRY

    Lap Heat Map vs Reference

    Your fastest lap drawn on the circuit, comparing speed deltas vs. the reference through each turn. For example: T5 is faster in, slower out; the reverse should be true. Shows where to find more speed next, at a glance.

  23. WELCOME

    PyFy Telemetry: F1 Performance Analysis

    Capture sim racing telemetry and turn it into practical, turn-by-turn/corner-by-corner insights you can use right away to gain lap time.
